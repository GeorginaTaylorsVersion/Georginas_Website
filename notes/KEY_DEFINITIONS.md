# Key Definitions and Formulas

## Integral

**What is an integral?**
**Define integral.**
**Meaning of integral.**

An **integral** is a mathematical concept that represents the area under a curve or the accumulation of quantities. The definite integral of a function $f(x)$ from $a$ to $b$ is written as:

$$
\int_a^b f(x) \, dx
$$

It is defined as the limit of Riemann sums and is used to calculate areas, volumes, and other quantities that accumulate continuously.
